# Strategic Naming Archetypes (David Placek Typologies)

Escaping the descriptive trap requires generating candidates across three non-descriptive territories.

## 1. Coined / Neologisms (Morphemic Inventions)
- **Mechanism:** Invented words constructed from phonesthemes and root morphemes.
- **Advantages:** 100% proprietary, pristine trademark clearance, blank cognitive slate.
- **Case Studies:**
  - *Vercel*: Conveys velocity and cloud acceleration.
  - *Sonos*: Symmetric ambigram signaling sound and balance.
  - *Pentium*: "Penta" (5th generation chip) + "-ium" (chemical element suffix for power).

## 2. Metaphorical / Arbitrary (Real Words in Novel Contexts)
- **Mechanism:** Existing dictionary words transplanted into unrelated categories to create psychological space.
- **Advantages:** Immediate human familiarity combined with unexpected category positioning.
- **Case Studies:**
  - *Azure*: Shifted enterprise cloud computing from dark server closets to serene, limitless blue sky.
  - *BlackBerry*: Evoked tactile keyboard buttons resembling berry drupelets, counteracting corporate email anxiety.
  - *Apple*: Disarmed sterile consumer computers with organic, approachable fruit.

## 3. Compound / Cognitive Multipliers
- **Mechanism:** Two evocative, contrasting words combined where $1 + 1 = 3$.
- **Advantages:** Tells a compound story; memorable imagery.
- **Case Studies:**
  - *Windsurf*: Effortless gliding over dynamic wind/waves (AI coding IDE).
  - *Firewall*: Physical architectural barrier mapped to cybersecurity.
  - *Snowflake*: Unique data crystals scaling effortlessly.
