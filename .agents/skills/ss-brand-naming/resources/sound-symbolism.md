# Sound Symbolism & Phonosemantics Guide

Reference guide based on David Placek's linguistic research at Lexicon Branding.

## Consonant Acoustics & Energy

### 1. Plosives / Stops (`B`, `P`, `D`, `T`, `K`, `G`)
- **Psychological Effect:** Crispness, explosive energy, physical presence, reliability.
- **Cognitive Association:** Solid structure, high performance, speed.
- **Iconic Precedents:** *BlackBerry*, *Pentium*, *PlayStation*, *PowerBook*.
- **Usage:** Ideal for foundational infrastructure, databases, hardware, high-throughput engines.

### 2. Fricatives (`V`, `F`, `S`, `Z`, `TH`)
- **Psychological Effect:** Frictionless motion, speed, elegance, continuous flow, lightness.
- **Cognitive Association:** Agility, swift transformation, ease of use.
- **Iconic Precedents:** *Vercel*, *Swiffer*, *Sonos*, *Fastly*.
- **Usage:** Ideal for developer platforms, frontend tooling, real-time collaboration, swift cloud runtimes.

### 3. Nasals & Liquids (`M`, `N`, `L`, `R`)
- **Psychological Effect:** Warmth, continuity, approachability, human connection.
- **Cognitive Association:** Memory, calmness, organic growth.
- **Iconic Precedents:** *Loom*, *Linear*, *Monzo*.

## Vowel Resonance

- **High Front Vowels (`i`, `e` - as in "pin", "fleet"):** Signal nimbleness, precision, micro-scale efficiency, sharpness.
- **Low Back Vowels (`a`, `o`, `u` - as in "vast", "nova", "summit"):** Signal scale, permanence, openness, gravitas, authority.
