## Capabilities & Output Artifacts

1. **Brand Strategy & Positioning:** Primary and secondary Jungian archetype mapping, core value proposition, audience personas, and positioning matrix (`docs/brand/brand-strategy.md`).
2. **Verbal Identity & Voice Sliders:** Formal vs. Playful, Technical vs. Accessible, Concise vs. Expressive tone spectrums with actionable microcopy dos and don'ts.
3. **Perceptually Uniform Color Systems:** OKLCH lightness, chroma, and hue calibration with built-in APCA/WCAG AA & AAA compliance scoring for both light and dark modes.
4. **Fluid Typography Scales:** Mathematical modular scale ratios (Major Third 1.250, Perfect Fourth 1.333, Golden Ratio 1.618) paired with high-legibility system and web fonts.
5. **Production Design Tokens:** Exported directly to W3C Community Group JSON (`docs/brand/brand-tokens.json`) and CSS custom properties (`docs/brand/brand-theme.css`).
6. **Living Interactive Brand Book:** Offline-capable, self-contained single-file HTML5/CSS application (`docs/brand/brand-book.html`) featuring live surface toggles, contrast auditors, and one-click token copiers.
