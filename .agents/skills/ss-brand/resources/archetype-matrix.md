# Jungian Archetype Matrix for Technology Products

Strategic mapping of the 12 classic archetypes to software brand identity, messaging tone, and UI paradigms.

---

## The 12 Archetypes

| Archetype | Core Desire | Brand Voice | UI / Aesthetic Style | Software Analogues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Sage** | Truth & understanding | Thoughtful, objective, precise, pedagogical | High typography contrast, generous margins, footnote clarity | Stripe, Notion, Wikipedia |
| **The Ruler** | Order & excellence | Authoritative, sovereign, uncompromising | Dense layouts, dark titanium palettes, keyboard shortcuts | Linear, AWS, Bloomberg |
| **The Creator** | Originality & enduring value | Expressive, inspiring, craft-focused | Canvas-first views, fluid drag-and-drop, rich color fidelity | Figma, Apple, Ableton |
| **The Magician** | Radical transformation | Visionary, effortless, magical, intuitive | Zero-config automations, glassmorphism, instant previews | Midjourney, Raycast, Vercel |
| **The Outlaw** | Liberation from status quo | Provocative, unapologetic, counter-cultural | Monospace typography, terminal aesthetics, radical transparency | PostHog, Ghost, Basecamp |
| **The Hero** | Mastery & overcoming obstacles | Resolute, disciplined, energizing, ambitious | Dynamic progress bars, telemetry metrics, performance badges | Strava, GitHub, Datadog |
| **The Everyman** | Belonging & unpretentious utility | Down-to-earth, supportive, honest, warm | Simple rounded cards, friendly illustrations, clear tooltips | Slack, Dropbox, Mailchimp |
| **The Caregiver** | Protection & nurture | Empathetic, safeguarding, calming, reassuring | Soft muted tones, reassuring confirmations, defensive undo states | 1Password, Loom, Headspace |
| **The Explorer** | Discovery & boundless frontier | Bold, curious, pioneering, autonomous | Global search shortcuts, infinite scroll, spatial graphs | Obsidian, Arc Browser, Docker |
| **The Jester** | Joy & lightheartedness | Playful, humorous, witty, self-aware | Easter eggs, expressive micro-animations, playful error states | Discord, Duolingo, Pitch |
| **The Lover** | Intimacy & sensory connection | Sensual, elegant, harmonious, refined | Micro-typography details, silky motion physics, bespoke icons | Readwise, Cron, Craft |
| **The Innocent** | Purity & simplicity | Optimistic, uncluttered, wholesome, sincere | Minimal borders, maximum natural light, distraction-free views | Bear Notes, Superhuman |

---

## Secondary "Shadow" Archetype Layering

Leading brands layer a **Primary Archetype (70%)** with a **Secondary Archetype (30%)** to create depth and prevent stereotype cliché:

- **Sage + Outlaw:** Supreme technical authority dismantling legacy lock-in (e.g. Linux, Git).
- **Ruler + Creator:** Institutional speed and keyboard control meeting exquisite micro-interactions (e.g. Linear).
- **Creator + Magician:** Visual canvas software that feels like immediate psychic manifestation (e.g. Figma).
