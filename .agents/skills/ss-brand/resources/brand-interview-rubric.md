# ss-brand — 5-Question Socratic Brand Interview Rubric

Structured interview guide for eliciting founder vision, brand archetype, aesthetic direction, and verbal voice.

---

## Rules of Engagement

- **One Question Per Turn:** Always ask one question at a time using `ask_question`.
- **Sensory & Emotional Grounding:** Guide founders away from generic terms ("modern", "clean") toward concrete aesthetic and emotional anchors.
- **Provide Curated Options with Recommendations:** Offer 3–4 distinct choices plus custom input for every dimension.

---

## The 5 Brand Dimensions

### Dimension 1: Brand Archetype & Core Character
- **Question:** "Which Jungian brand archetype best embodies your product's fundamental mission and relationship with its users?"
- **Options:**
  - `(Recommended) The Sage / Mentor — Authoritative, empirical, truth-seeking, clarity-first (e.g. Stripe, Notion)`
  - `The Creator / Innovator — Expressive, visionary, empowering craftsmanship (e.g. Figma, Apple)`
  - `The Ruler / Architect — High-performance, institutional, uncompromising control (e.g. Linear, AWS)`
  - `The Outlaw / Rebel — Provocative, disruptive, challenging status-quo orthodoxy (e.g. PostHog, Basecamp)`
- **Elicitation:** Primary archetype, secondary shadow archetype, core product promise.

---

### Dimension 2: Aesthetic Direction & Visual Atmosphere
- **Question:** "What visual design philosophy aligns closest with your product experience?"
- **Options:**
  - `(Recommended) Precision Minimalist (Swiss) — High density, neutral surfaces, crisp borders, micro-interactions`
  - `Warm Editorial — Generous whitespace, organic earthy undertones, sophisticated typography`
  - `Cyber-Industrial — Deep dark mode, high-contrast neon accents, monospace cues, technical telemetry`
  - `Neo-Brutalist — Bold geometric contrast, raw borders, playful high-saturation accents`
- **Elicitation:** Border radii preferences, surface contrast levels, density expectations.

---

### Dimension 3: Color Mood & Chromatic Identity
- **Question:** "What primary chromatic mood best projects your category position?"
- **Options:**
  - `(Recommended) Electric Sapphire & Slate — Precision, security, deep intelligence (Hue ~260°)`
  - `Emerald & Obsidian — Growth, stability, velocity, algorithmic power (Hue ~155°)`
  - `Amber & Carbon — Warmth, craft, energy, industrial endurance (Hue ~45°)`
  - `Monochromatic Titanium — Pure tonal sophistication with high-contrast functional badges (Hue ~0°)`
- **Elicitation:** Brand primary hue, secondary functional hues (success, warning, error, info), dark mode default.

---

### Dimension 4: Typography Personality & Rhythm
- **Question:** "What typographic voice should represent your product across headings and interface copy?"
- **Options:**
  - `(Recommended) Geometric Sans (Modernist) — Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans paired with JetBrains Mono`
  - `Humanist Sans (Approachable) — Geist / Public Sans paired with Fira Code`
  - `Editorial Hybrid — Merriweather / Playfair display headings with clean sans-serif UI`
  - `Technical Monospace Lead — Monaspace / IBM Plex Mono prominent for developer focus`
- **Elicitation:** Modular scale ratio (1.200 Minor Third vs 1.250 Major Third vs 1.333 Perfect Fourth).

---

### Dimension 5: Tone of Voice Sliders & Verbal Identity
- **Question:** "Where does your brand communication fall along the following 4 voice sliders?"
- **Sliders:**
  1. **Formal (0) ⟷ Casual (10):** Target default 4 (Professional, respectful, but conversational).
  2. **Technical (0) ⟷ Accessible (10):** Target default 6 (Explains complexity with plain clarity).
  3. **Concise (0) ⟷ Narrative (10):** Target default 2 (Laser-efficient microcopy, zero fluff).
  4. **Reserved (0) ⟷ Energetic (10):** Target default 5 (Confident, calm, quietly competent).
- **Elicitation:** Error message tone, empty state personality, call-to-action phrasing philosophy.
