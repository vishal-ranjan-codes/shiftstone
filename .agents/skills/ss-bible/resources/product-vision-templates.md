```markdown
# Product Vision & Strategy — Vision & Mission

## 1. The Founding Thesis
The platform was built to remove friction from complex workflows through intelligent automation and robust distributed infrastructure.

## 2. The Trigger Moment
The exact moment a user adopts the platform is when existing manual processes cause unacceptable error rates and operational bottlenecks.

## 3. The 3-Year Trajectory
From core foundational capability to an extensible industry standard with third-party developer integrations.
```

```markdown
# Product Vision & Strategy — Product Philosophy

## 1. Operating Covenants
- Anti-Predatory Covenant: User privacy is sacred; data is never monetized or exposed.
- Portability Covenant: Users retain total ownership and can export data at any time.

## 2. Ergonomic Simplicity
Every feature must earn its place by demonstrably reducing cognitive load for the user.
```
